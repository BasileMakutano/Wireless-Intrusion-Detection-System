What do these mean?

n_events / n_dis: association and disassociation events from one device in one second. dis_ratio: share that are disassociations. min_gap: shortest time between two events (attacks are milliseconds apart). n_10s / n_60s: events from that device in the last 10 / 60 seconds. gap_prev: seconds since its previous event (normal devices wait long; attackers don't).
